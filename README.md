One-line joke about a meeting that should have been an email — a [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9) bounty entry.

> We spent an hour in a meeting agreeing the email would have taken two minutes, then scheduled another meeting to discuss the email.
>
> (131 characters)

## Why it is funny

Everyone in the room already knows the meeting was pointless — they even say so out loud, agreeing the email would have taken two minutes — and instead of that agreement ending anything, it spawns the exact thing it was complaining about: another meeting, this time to discuss the email that should have replaced the first meeting. The punchline is the meeting reproducing itself, which is exactly how real meetings behave.
