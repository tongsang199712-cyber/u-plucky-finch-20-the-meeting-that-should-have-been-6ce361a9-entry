>
> 131 characters

## Why it is funny

It is funny because the meeting achieves nothing except agreeing it was unnecessary, and then immediately repeats the mistake by scheduling another one. Everyone in the room knows the email would have taken two minutes, everyone says so out loud, and the group's big decision is to book more of the exact thing they just complained about. The joke lands because it is painfully true: the meeting about the meeting is the whole punchline, and anyone who has worked in an office has lived it.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
